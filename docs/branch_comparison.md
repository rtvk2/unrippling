**Metrics and leakage.** Both internal metric implementations support data_range=1; perfect agreement gives infinite PSNR and SSIM 1. Both VGG LPIPS paths rescale image values from [0,1] to [−1,1]. The main sweep additionally computes full RGB quantized metrics and Alex LPIPS. Its sweep uses 199-frame physics clips and can time concurrently on the CPU/GPU. Our tables use 100-frame grayscale public clips and a four-pixel crop. Main’s oracle selection and GT-start reference experiments are clearly diagnostic in their scripts; their scores are unsuitable as deployable reconstructions. Its development backgrounds differ from the sweep backgrounds, but shallow profile_6 recurs across splits. Its 128 tuples use only four independent background identities. Surviving prose does not establish every actual tuning decision.

**The 42 GB claim.** Zenodo record 19390086 contains three parts of 12,884,901,888 bytes and one of 5,158,536,495 bytes, totaling 43,813,242,159 bytes (40.8 GiB). It supplies backgrounds and wave normals. The record has no explicit semantic version. Main reports that the four parts were downloaded and MD5-verified on Windows and that two 200-map profiles per wave type were extracted, about 0.59 GB per profile. Later diagnostic work used additional profiles. Total extracted bytes were not evidenced. These files, checksums from that machine, and its extraction logs are absent from the checked-out tree. [Dataset record](https://zenodo.org/records/19390086)

Main's project log reports 30 backgrounds, four wave families, ten profiles per family and four amplitudes, giving 4,800 possible generated conditions. It reports 60 selected background/profile pairs per family and amplitude, giving 960 official conditions. The archived subset index was not available here to independently enumerate them. The official evaluator constructs RGB clips and compares to the clean background, with full-frame TorchMetrics PSNR/SSIM and VGG/Alex LPIPS. These are generated clips rather than 4,800 separately recorded videos. The repository GIFs are demonstration composites. [Official code](https://github.com/iafoss/refractive-mfir-benchmark/tree/45eee65f291eb4cdd82987f51af3ad850f130d47)

**What main actually supports.** scripts/run_sweep.py fixes backgrounds 8, 10, 12, 14; profiles 6 and 10; shallow, ocean, ripple and sine waves; low, mid, high and extreme amplitudes. This gives 128 × 199 = 25,472 generated input frame instances at 512 square, shared among methods, for a complete intended sweep. Prose reports about 3.2 h, a GPU OOM at clip 120 followed by resumption, a 32-clip protocol subset, and separate James runs on 14 videos. Do not add the overlapping subsets to infer unique coverage. The prose's claimed official overlap includes background 11, outside the sweep’s hardcoded background list. The exact overlap is unverified.
